# Sticky-notes-to-do-lists

**About This Board**
**Dual Purpose:** It combines free form text notes with structured checklists, allowing you to "pin your thoughts" and "tick your tasks" in one place.
**Real-Time Editing:** You don't need to open a separate window. You can click any text in a sticky note or to-do list to start typing and editing immediately.
**Smart Organization:** The top search bar and filter buttons ("All," "Notes," "To-dos") give you the power to quickly find specific items once your board starts to fill up.
**Auto-Save:** You never have to worry about hitting a save button; the app automatically saves your changes directly in your browser.
**Visual Customization:** You can color-code your thoughts to organize them visually.

**How to Add & Delete**
➕ Adding Items:
**New Notes/Lists:** Click the "+ Sticky note" or "+ To-do list" buttons in the top right toolbar.
**New Tasks:** Inside a blue To-do list, type your task into the "Add an item..." field at the bottom and click the "Add" button.
🗑️ Deleting Items:
**Remove Entire Notes/Lists:** Look for the small trash can icon located in the bottom-right corner of each note (next to the date). Click it to delete the whole card.
**Remove Specific Tasks**: If you are in a To-do list, each item has a small "X" next to it. Click that "X" to remove just that single task without deleting the whole list.
